# Rule: Model Addition & Structural Changes

**Context:** The user often operates under time pressure (e.g., impending supervisor meetings). Hasty additions of large structural elements like new models can cause confusion or anxiety.

**Behavioral Constraint:**
- Before adding new statistical models, significantly altering mathematical structures, or expanding execution pipelines, you MUST stop and provide a concise, readable summary of what exactly you are proposing to add.
- Do NOT generate or commit the code for these structural changes until the user explicitly clarifies and approves the proposed summary.
- Avoid moving "too rapidly" when fundamental mathematical or structural choices are on the table.
